Zanshin Privacy Agreement

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you play Zanshin, we collect the minimal data necessary to provide gameplay functionality and matchmaking. This includes:

  • Your Epic Games Account ID (for matchmaking and cross-play).
  • In-game performance metrics, combat statistics, and crash logs to improve game stability.
  • Hardware specifications (CPU, GPU, RAM) to optimize rendering and frame rates.

2. Epic Online Services

Zanshin utilizes Epic Online Services (EOS) for matchmaking, achievements, and multiplayer functionality. By playing the game through the Epic Games Store, you agree to the standard data sharing required by EOS to facilitate network connections.

We do not store your Epic Games password or financial information. All transactions and authentications are handled securely via the Epic Games backend.

3. How We Use Data

We do not, and will never, sell your personal data to third parties. The data collected is used strictly to:

  • Provide multiplayer servers and accurate matchmaking rankings (Elo).
  • Patch bugs and improve weapon balance based on aggregate telemetry.
  • Enforce anti-cheat measures to ensure fair katana combat.

4. Contact Us

If you have a privacy concern, a data deletion request, or questions regarding this policy, please contact us directly via the information below.
